Terms

Last updated: 23 August 2026

Educational purpose

AUREN is designed as a self-study aid. It does not replace official examination authorities, schools, teachers or professional academic guidance.

Accuracy

Official sources are preferred for syllabi, examination notices and other high-stakes academic facts. AI-generated explanations and questions must be clearly treated as generated content and may contain errors.

Accounts

You are responsible for protecting access to your account and for providing accurate information. Do not attempt to access another student's account or data.

Content

Do not upload or distribute material you do not have permission to use. Do not submit harmful, illegal, abusive or malicious content.

Tests and rankings

AUREN must never manufacture test results, ranks, percentiles or participant counts. Rankings are meaningful only when calculated from real, authorized attempts under published rules.

Availability

Features may be unavailable while their secure backend implementation is being completed. An unavailable feature must not be represented as functioning through fake data.

Changes

These terms may be updated as the service moves from prototype to production. Material changes should be communicated through the service.
