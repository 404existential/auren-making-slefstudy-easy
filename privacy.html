Privacy

Last updated: 23 August 2026

What AUREN collects

AUREN may process account information, authentication information, academic preferences, study activity, test attempts, uploaded learning material and content you intentionally submit. We only use information necessary to provide and secure the service.

Authentication

Authentication is handled by Supabase Auth. Passwords are not stored by the AUREN frontend. Email-code authentication is subject to the email provider and Supabase Auth configuration.

Academic data

Your academic activity should be treated as private by default. We do not present invented scores, rankings, syllabus information or study material as fact.

Uploaded material

Only upload material you are permitted to use. AUREN should not be used to distribute copyrighted material without permission.

AI

AI features will only be enabled through a server-side provider configuration. Secret provider API keys must never be placed in browser code. AI output can be wrong and must not be treated as an official examination authority.

Retention and deletion

Data retention, deletion and account-removal controls will be implemented as the production backend is completed. Until those controls are available, AUREN should not be treated as a fully launched service for sensitive academic records.

Security

We use authentication controls, database row-level security, server-side secrets for future AI services, input validation and content-security controls. Security is continuously reviewed and no website can honestly guarantee zero risk.

Contact

For privacy requests, use the contact method published by the operator when the production service is launched.
